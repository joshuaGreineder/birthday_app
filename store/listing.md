## Full description

4000 characters maximum.

```
Happaroo reminds you of birthdays, anniversaries, and the other dates you want to keep.

Add a name, a month and day, and a group. If you know the year, Happaroo can show how old someone is turning or which anniversary it is. If you do not know the year, it stores the month and day only and hides the age. It does not fill in a year. February 29 stays February 29.

On this device you can:

- See who is coming up on the home list, a month-by-month timeline, and a calendar
- Filter by groups you name yourself. A blank group is shown as Ungrouped
- Make your own event types
- Turn on reminders for the days you choose. Notifications are shown by the phone
- Export a JSON or CSV backup, and import one after a preview of what will be added
- Copy birthdays from the contacts on this phone, only when you ask

Your names, dates, notes, and groups are not uploaded to a Happaroo account. There is no account. There are no ads and no analytics. The privacy policy is at https://happaroo.com/privacy.

Happaroo is a reminder you keep yourself. It is not a shared calendar. Calendar sync is off in this version.
```
