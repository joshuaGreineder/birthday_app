Wireless Debugging Quick Reference
====================================

1. PAIR your device (first time only):
   $env:ANDROID_HOME = "C:\Users\jgreineder\AppData\Local\Android\sdk"
   & "$env:ANDROID_HOME\platform-tools\adb.exe" pair <IP>:<PAIRING_PORT>
   (Enter the 6-digit code when prompted)

2. CONNECT to your device:
   & "$env:ANDROID_HOME\platform-tools\adb.exe" connect <IP>:<CONNECTION_PORT>

3. CHECK connected devices:
   & "$env:ANDROID_HOME\platform-tools\adb.exe" devices
   OR
   flutter devices

4. DISCONNECT when done:
   & "$env:ANDROID_HOME\platform-tools\adb.exe" disconnect <IP>:<PORT>

5. DEPLOY and see logs:
   flutter run

Note: The pairing port and connection port are DIFFERENT!
- Pairing port: shown when you tap "Pair device with pairing code"
- Connection port: shown on the main Wireless Debugging screen
