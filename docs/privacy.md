# Privacy policy

Canonical URL: [https://happaroo.com/privacy](https://happaroo.com/privacy)

Happaroo is a birthday and event reminder. This policy describes what the app does with information.

## Information that stays on your device

Happaroo stores the names, dates, and notes you enter, along with custom event labels, colors, reminder choices, and your theme. That information is kept in a local database on the device (Hive). A home-screen widget may keep a copy of the next event's name and countdown on the device.

Happaroo has no accounts and no server of its own.

## Contacts

If you choose Import from contacts, the app asks for permission to read contacts. It reads names and birthdays on the device so you can pick which ones to save. Happaroo does not upload your contacts. If you decline, the import does not run, and you can still add events yourself.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on the device. They are not sent through a push service. Happaroo asks for notification permission when you turn reminders on. If notifications are not allowed, reminders will not appear. You can allow them later in the system settings for Happaroo.

## Google Calendar

This version does not sync with Google Calendar. The app includes calendar-sync code that is switched off. While it is off, Happaroo does not sign you in to Google and does not send event names, notes, or dates to Google.

## Analytics, advertising, and selling data

Happaroo does not include analytics. It does not show ads. It does not sell your information, and it does not share it with third parties.

## Backup

Happaroo does not turn off Android backup. If backup is enabled for the device, Android may include the app's local files in the device backup. That backup belongs to the device account. It is not a Happaroo account, and the app does not add its own encryption.

## Deleting information

You can delete individual events in the app. Clear all data in Settings deletes saved events. Uninstalling Happaroo removes the app's local data from the device. There is no server copy to delete.
