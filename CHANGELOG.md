# Changelog

## 2.0.0

First Play upload. versionName **2.0.0**, versionCode **3** (`2.0.0+3` in `pubspec.yaml`).

- Birthdays, anniversaries, and custom event types, stored on the device with Hive.
- A month and day can be saved with no year. Age and anniversary counts stay hidden, and the sentinel year is not shown.
- Home, timeline, and calendar, plus groups (a blank group is Ungrouped).
- On-device reminders. JSON and CSV backup with an import preview.
- Material 3, light and dark, with a skippable first-run onboarding.
- Calendar sync stays off. No accounts, analytics, or ads.
